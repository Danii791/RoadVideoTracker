Shows coordinates, heading, speed, and elevation. Uses the mpv player with JSON IPC for playback; on Windows mpv is downloaded automatically on first use and cached in the QGIS settings folder. If mpv cannot be downloaded or is not available, playback falls back to the built-in Qt Multimedia engine.
